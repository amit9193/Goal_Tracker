How it works

Set. Track. Review.

A simple rhythm that turns annual goals into quarterly momentum.

Set meaningful goals

Agree each goal with its owner: the description, a measurable KPI, a start date and a target date.

Plan quarterly targets

Break the goal into planned progress for each quarter so expectations are explicit from day one.

Review & recalibrate

Log actual achievement every quarter. Variance and status highlight where to coach, unblock or celebrate.

Transparent methodology

The same formulas your spreadsheet uses — applied consistently

No black boxes. KPI Tracker follows the exact calculation rules of your Goal Tracker workbook, so numbers always reconcile.

  • {% include "icons.html" with i="check" %}Achievement % = Actual Achieved ÷ Planned Target, per quarter
  • {% include "icons.html" with i="check" %}Variance = Planned − Actual, flagged when you fall behind
  • {% include "icons.html" with i="check" %}Status: Not Started · On Track · Slow · At Risk · Complete
// Quarterly achievement — per goal
achievement = actual / planned
variance    = planned − actual
